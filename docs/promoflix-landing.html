Do I have to switch software?+

No. We work with Jobber, Housecall Pro, ServiceTitan or QuickBooks, or from a simple customer list if you don’t use any of them.

Is giving you manager access safe?+

Yes. You stay the owner of your Google profile. A manager can’t remove you, lock you out or transfer the profile, and you can remove our access in one click.

What happens if I get a bad review?+

Every customer gets the same review request. We never pick and choose who is asked, which is what keeps this within Google’s rules and FTC guidance. When a low rating comes in, we let you know right away and have a calm, professional reply drafted for you to approve.

Who writes the review replies?+

We draft them with AI set up for your business and services. Anything sensitive, like a complaint, a health detail or an employee’s name, is held for a person to review before it posts.

Are the texts compliant?+

Yes. Your texting is registered with the carriers (A2P 10DLC), every message includes a way to opt out, and we only text customers who have agreed to hear from you.

How do I cancel?+

Send us a note before your next billing date. There’s no contract and no cancellation fee, and your reviews stay on your profile.
